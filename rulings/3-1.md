Two entries of one kind can't share a name, and a commission can't make an entry with the name of one in the catalog or one an open commission makes. It can use that one instead (701.4). If W4 is open and makes an item named Flower, a second Flower item from this commission is not allowed. Calling it a duplicate is right, though 701.4 is the reason, not 703.3. Gremlin's post, which a model drafted, cites 703.3, but that clause is about attributes, not names. As the ticket stands now, it makes only a rule, and that rule names the Flower by its commission, as 703.4 allows. So nothing is duplicated, and the rule points at W4's Flower, which is what you asked for. Three things follow. First, the catalog has no Flower yet. Until W4's Flower is built, this rule has nothing to act on (703.4), and no Flower is on the board unless a rule puts it there (701.3). Second, the rule relies on W4 making a Flower. If W4 isn't open or makes no item by that name, the rule names something the game doesn't have (103.9). The records before me don't show W4, so this ruling doesn't settle whether it does. Third, an item needs the rule that gives it to you (704.1). The Flower can go live without that rule (704.3), but until a rule gives Flowers to players, nobody has one to give. Also, a creature with no hearts in the catalog can't lose any (601.2), so a hostile creature with no hearts eats the Flower and loses nothing.
