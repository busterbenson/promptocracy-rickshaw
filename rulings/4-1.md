No rule defines owning a creature, so "its owner" in 606.1 is read by what made her. A catalog entry comes in by commission (701.2), and a commission is a player's proposal (801.1). Pancake came in by W2, so her owner is the player who asked for W2: its requester. She moves toward that player's piece, wherever it is. This ruling reads 606.1 only. It doesn't say who owns anything else, and it gives the owner no other power over her. A proposal can change it, or an appeal can (301). It was made with the operator build of W2 on day 4, which Buster asked for that morning, not at night as 106.5 usually has it.
